Glaucoma progression can be difficult to recognize in color fundus photographs. We developed RetinaProgress, a geometry-first framework that simulates five-year optic-cup enlargement while preserving original fundus texture. Official longitudinal GRAPE contours supervised smooth time-conditioned deformation, and PAPILA initialized disc/cup segmentation. On a held-out eye, generated VCDR increased 0.109 while outer-disc Dice remained 0.966. A blinded web reader instrument was built for subsequent clinician evaluation.
