# Draft request for IRB or institutional determination

## Project title

Blinded clinician assessment of diffusion-generated longitudinal fundus-image
scenarios

## Request

Please determine whether this activity is exempt human-subjects research,
non-human-subjects research, or requires another review pathway.

## Activity

Eye-care professionals will view 20 de-identified fundus-image cases in a web
application and record image assessments. The application collects optional
non-identifying reviewer code, specialty, years of experience, case responses,
confidence, elapsed time, and optional comments. It instructs reviewers not to
enter names, email addresses, or patient information.

## Image data

- GRAPE public research dataset: established-glaucoma longitudinal fundus
  photographs.
- PAPILA public research dataset: cross-sectional healthy fundus photographs
  used only as unchanged stability controls.
- Diffusion-generated images derived from these de-identified photographs.

No clinical records will be accessed through the reader application. No
patient-facing decisions will be made.

## Participants

Adult optometrists and ophthalmologists recruited by professional email.
Participation is voluntary. No compensation is currently planned.

## Data

The system stores a random session identifier, optional non-identifying reviewer
code, professional background, years of experience, case ratings, timing, and
comments. IP addresses are not intentionally stored by the study database.
Results will be reported in aggregate.

## Risks

Risks are expected to be minimal and primarily involve professional time and
the unlikely disclosure of reviewer identity if a reviewer enters identifying
text despite instructions.

## Safeguards

- No patient information is requested.
- Reviewer names and email addresses are not collected in the study form.
- Free-text fields include a warning against identifiable information.
- Access to the server-side answer key and response database is restricted.
- The application states that the model is not validated for clinical use.

## Outputs

Aggregate measures of image detectability, disease-class preservation, healthy
false-progression ratings, reader agreement, and qualitative failure modes may
be included in a student research presentation or manuscript.
