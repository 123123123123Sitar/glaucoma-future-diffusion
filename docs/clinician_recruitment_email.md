# Clinician recruitment email draft

**Subject:** Invitation: blinded review of simulated longitudinal fundus images

Dear Dr. [Last Name],

I am a high school student conducting a research project with mentorship from
Dr. Gundolf Schenk at the UCSF Bakar Computational Health Sciences Institute.
We are evaluating a research-only diffusion model that creates simulated
follow-up color fundus images.

We are inviting optometrists and ophthalmologists who review fundus photographs
to complete a blinded 20-case study. Each case includes a baseline image and two
candidates. You will assess image realism, normal-versus-glaucoma appearance,
and possible structural progression. The review takes approximately 15–20
minutes.

The system is not a clinical diagnostic or prognostic tool. Images are
de-identified research data, and the study does not request patient
information. Please do not enter your name or patient details in the response
fields.

Before recruitment begins, we will provide the applicable institutional
human-subjects/IRB determination and any required consent language.

If you are interested, please reply to this email and I will send the study
link and study-information sheet.
